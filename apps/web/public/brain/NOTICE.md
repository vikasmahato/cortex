# Brain model attribution

`brain.bin` / `brain.json` are generated by `tools/build_brain.py` from the **FreeSurfer `fsaverage`**
cortical surface template (pial + white surfaces, `fsaverage6` resolution), obtained via
[nilearn](https://nilearn.github.io) `fetch_surf_fsaverage`. fsaverage is an average of real MRI scans
distributed with FreeSurfer (https://surfer.nmr.mgh.harvard.edu/fswiki/FreeSurferWiki?action=LicenseTerms).

What is real vs. approximated:
- Cortex shape and folds: real (fsaverage).
- Lobe/region boundaries on the cortex: approximated from anatomical landmarks (central sulcus, Sylvian fissure).
- Hippocampus, amygdala, cerebellum, brainstem: procedurally placed (not in the cortical template).

Regenerate:  python tools/build_brain.py <nilearn_data_dir> apps/web/public/brain 18000   (needs nilearn, nibabel, numpy)
